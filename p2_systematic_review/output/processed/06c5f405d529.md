## Abstract summary
This industry whitepaper explores the application of quantum computing to enhance employee well-being and financial decision-making by enabling personalized healthcare, optimized financial planning, and improved organizational resource allocation. It highlights relevant quantum algorithms (VQE, QAOA, QMC), their potential computational advantages over classical methods, and discusses NISQ-era hardware limitations alongside proposed hybrid approaches and future research directions.
## Methodology
This industry whitepaper adopts a conceptual and exploratory methodology rather than an empirical or experimental one. The authors review quantum computing fundamentals and survey quantum algorithms relevant to health and financial decision-making (e.g., VQE, QAOA, QMC, QSVM, QNN), then map these algorithms to application scenarios such as personalized healthcare modeling, drug interaction simulation, portfolio optimization, retirement planning, debt management and organizational scheduling. The paper analyzes computational complexity and the potential for quantum advantage, discusses NISQ-era constraints (limited qubits, noise), and outlines proposed hybrid quantum-classical approaches, error mitigation, and future research directions. Two high-level case vignettes are presented (a multinational corporation reducing stress-related absences and a financial services firm improving retirement planning outcomes), but no experimental protocols, datasets, or quantitative evaluation procedures are provided. The methodology is therefore literature- and concept-driven, combining algorithmic mapping, theoretical complexity discussion, and illustrative (non-reproducible) case summaries to motivate future empirical work.
