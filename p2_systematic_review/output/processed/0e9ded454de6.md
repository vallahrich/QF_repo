## Abstract summary
This theoretical paper examines how quantum computing can transform financial technologies and digital asset ecosystems by enabling faster, parallelized computations for tasks like market forecasting, portfolio optimization, and fraud detection. It discusses specific quantum algorithms (QAOA, QFT, Grover) and quantum-resistant cryptography, and advocates hybrid quantum–classical frameworks to improve security, transaction speed, and systemic resilience in FinTech and DeFi environments.
