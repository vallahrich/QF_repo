## Abstract summary
This paper proposes CryptoQNet, a quantum computing framework that combines Quantum Feature Maps, Variational Quantum Circuits, and a Quantum Recurrent Neural Network to forecast cryptocurrency prices and volatility. Evaluated on Bitcoin and Ethereum data (via quantum simulators), CryptoQNet reports lower MAE/RMSE and MAPE than classical LSTM and GRU baselines, while noting limitations in hardware availability, training overhead, and scalability and suggesting future hybrid quantum-classical and real-hardware work.
## Methodology
The study proposes CryptoQNet, a hybrid quantum-classical pipeline for cryptocurrency time-series forecasting that encodes preprocessed historical market data into quantum states and processes them with parameterized quantum circuits. Classical input vectors (features: open, high, low, close, volume, market cap and derived indicators such as moving averages and volatility) are normalized and amplitude-encoded into quantum states. Quantum Feature Maps (QFM) map the encoded data into a higher-dimensional Hilbert space via a parameterized unitary U_phi. A Variational Quantum Circuit (VQC) ansatz composed of repeated blocks of Rz and Rx rotation gates and CNOT entangling gates (notation: U(θ) = ∏k Rz(θk) Rx(θk) CNOT) is applied; the output quantum state is measured to obtain classical probabilities. Training minimizes a cross-entropy loss using classical optimizers (Adam or RMSProp) with gradients computed by the parameter-shift rule. For temporal modeling, a Quantum Recurrent Neural Network (QRNN) is used — a quantum analogue of LSTM gates (forget, input, output, cell updates) parameterized by quantum gate parameters — to capture sequential dependencies. Performance is evaluated against classical baselines (LSTM, GRU) using MAE, RMSE and MAPE; feature importance is inferred from quantum measurement results. Experiments were run on a quantum simulator (unnamed) and compared in training and inference time against classical models.

**Algorithms used:** Amplitude Encoding, Quantum Feature Map (QFM), Variational Quantum Circuit (VQC), Quantum Recurrent Neural Network (QRNN) / quantum-LSTM analogue, Parameter-shift rule (for gradients), Classical optimizers: Adam, RMSProp

**Experimental setup:** Experiments were executed on a quantum simulator (unnamed). Execution time comparisons were performed between CryptoQNet on the simulator and classical models under equivalent classical settings. No specific simulator name, QPU model, or cloud provider is reported.

**Dataset:** Historical Bitcoin and Ethereum market data including open, high, low, close prices, trading volume, market capitalization across multiple years. Derived indicators (moving averages, volatility) were created. Exact data source and dataset size are not specified.
