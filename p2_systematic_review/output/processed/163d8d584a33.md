## Abstract summary
The paper proposes a hybrid Quantum LSTM (QLSTM) that integrates a classical LSTM encoder with a quantum neural network (QNN) layer to improve time-series forecasting in dynamic financial markets. Multiple quantum feature maps and ansatz circuit designs are evaluated, and the tuned QLSTM (e.g., ZZFeatureMap with RealAmplitudes) is reported to outperform a classical LSTM on Apple stock data, yielding lower errors and higher R². The study highlights design choices for NISQ-era circuits and hybrid optimization to capture complex nonlinear patterns with fewer parameters.
## Methodology
The study constructs a hybrid quantum-classical forecasting pipeline for daily Apple (AAPL) stock prices. Historical OHLCV data was obtained from Yahoo Finance (via the yfinance library) covering June 3, 2019 to May 30, 2025 (≈1,508 trading days). After exploratory data analysis, preprocessing retained Open, High, Low, Close and Volume columns, removed non-numeric/missing rows, normalized values to [0,1], and used a sliding-window scheme (sequence length = 20 days) to predict the next-day Close. The data were split chronologically (48% train, 32% validation, 20% test). The model pipeline consists of a classical LSTM (hidden size 16) ingesting 20-day sequences, a dense projection reducing the LSTM hidden state to a 4-dimensional vector with tanh activation, and a Quantum Neural Network (QNN) quantum layer operating on 4 qubits. The QNN encodes inputs with a feature map (best reported: ZZFeatureMap with 2 repetitions) and applies a parametrized ansatz (best reported: RealAmplitudes, two-layer, full entanglement). The quantum layer outputs are passed to a final classical dense layer for regression. Training used RMSprop minimizing MSE with early stopping (reported best configuration: 100 epochs, learning rate 0.01). Multiple feature-map/ansatz combinations were compared (e.g., ZZFeatureMap+RealAmplitudes, PauliFeatureMap+RealAmplitudes, EfficientSU2 variants) and evaluated with MSE, MAE, RMSE, R^2 and Explained Variance to select the optimal quantum circuit. The QLSTM hybrid was benchmarked against a purely classical LSTM baseline.

**Algorithms used:** LSTM, Hybrid Quantum-Classical QNN (QLSTM), ZZFeatureMap, PauliFeatureMap, ZFeatureMap, RealAmplitudes ansatz, EfficientSU2 ansatz, Sliding-window time-series preprocessing, RMSprop optimizer, tanh activation

**Dataset:** Daily Apple Inc. (AAPL) historical stock data from Yahoo Finance (via yfinance), covering June 3, 2019 to May 30, 2025; approximately 1,508 trading-day observations with Open, High, Low, Close, Adjusted Close and Volume.

**Results summary:** The paper proposes and empirically evaluates a hybrid QLSTM model that couples a classical LSTM encoder with a 4‑qubit parametrized quantum neural network. Using ≈1,508 daily AAPL observations and a 20‑day input window, the authors report that the QLSTM (with ZZFeatureMap + RealAmplitudes) achieves substantially better regression performance than a classical LSTM baseline (reported R² 0.83 vs 0.54), lower error metrics, and smoother training/validation loss curves. They also report that other feature map / ansatz combinations produced much worse performance, highlighting sensitivity to circuit design. The authors frame the approach as suitable for NISQ-era variational circuits and claim improved expressivity and generalization from the quantum layer, while noting practical challenges around circuit depth and stability.

## Quantum advantage claim
**Classification:** speculative

The authors report improved predictive metrics for their QLSTM versus a classical LSTM on a single AAPL dataset and identify specific quantum circuit choices that worked best in their experiments. However, the claimed advantage is based on a limited empirical study (small-scale 4‑qubit QNN, likely simulated variational circuits) and does not constitute a general, reproducible demonstration of quantum advantage on real quantum hardware or across diverse datasets. Therefore the claim of quantum advantage remains speculative rather than broadly demonstrated.
