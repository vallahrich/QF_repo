This chapter reviews fundamentals of quantum computing, focusing on qubits, quantum gates, and the theoretical principles of superposition and entanglement. It discusses practical challenges such as decoherence and error correction, surveys implementations and performance considerations, and outlines potential applications across finance, healthcare, logistics, and materials science.
