## Abstract summary
The paper proposes a Quantum Neural Network (QNN) framework for time series forecasting that encodes classical time series into quantum states and processes them with parameterized quantum circuits to capture complex temporal dependencies. The authors describe data encoding (including amplitude encoding), circuit design and training, and report experimental evaluations (on ECG datasets) showing improved predictive metrics compared with classical baselines.
## Methodology
The authors propose a hybrid quantum-classical approach for time series forecasting using a Quantum Neural Network (QNN) framework (referred to as QNNTST). The methodology begins with classical data preprocessing (data cleaning, normalization, optional feature extraction and a temporal train/test split). Classical time series vectors are encoded into quantum states via quantum feature mapping, with amplitude encoding discussed as the primary embedding technique (mapping values to rotation angles / amplitudes). The QNN architecture is described at a conceptual level: parameterized quantum circuits (variational quantum circuits) constitute the quantum layers (quantum neurons and gates such as Hadamard, Pauli and controlled gates are mentioned) while classical layers handle preprocessing/postprocessing and parameter optimization. Training is performed by optimizing gate parameters to minimize a prediction loss (classical optimizer over circuit parameters), measurements of qubits produce outputs which are interpreted classically to yield forecasts. The experimental evaluation compares the proposed QNNTST against baseline classifiers (WCTSD and MSVM) on benchmark ECG time-series datasets, using MATLAB for implementations and reporting accuracy, precision, recall and F-measure for comparison.

**Algorithms used:** Quantum Neural Network (QNN), QNNTST (Quantum Neural Network based Time Series Forecasting), Amplitude encoding (quantum feature mapping), Variational Quantum Circuit (parameterized quantum circuit), WCTSD (Weak Classifier aware Time Series Data Classification) - baseline, MSVM (Multi-class SVM or variant) - baseline
