## Abstract summary
This paper reviews methods for applying quantum computing to optimization problems across domains such as logistics, finance, and resource management, focusing on algorithms that can outperform classical approaches. It discusses Grover's search, variational quantum algorithms (notably QAOA), quantum stochastic search, and hybrid classical-quantum approaches, and reports on simulation and validation using platforms like IBM Q, Google Cirq, and Microsoft QDK, highlighting potential speedups for combinatorial and discrete optimization tasks while noting the need for further research and practical implementation.
## Methodology
