## Abstract summary
The paper proposes an AI–quantum hybrid framework that combines quantum machine learning algorithms (e.g., QSVM, QNNs) with classical deep learning to improve organizational risk forecasting and adaptive crisis response across financial, operational, and reputational domains. Empirical simulations indicate the hybrid approach better identifies latent crisis patterns, manages uncertainty propagation, and optimizes mitigation strategies, while emphasizing interpretability, data security, and ethical governance for quantum-ready resilience frameworks.

The study proposes and evaluates an AI–quantum hybrid framework for organizational risk forecasting and crisis management.
