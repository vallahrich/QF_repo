## Abstract summary
This book provides a comprehensive introduction to quantum computing, covering the mathematical foundations, quantum subsystems and operations, programming and simulators, and a wide range of quantum algorithms. It emphasizes practical, real-world applications—including cryptography, optimization, machine learning, and solutions for transportation, scheduling, fraud detection, and smart cities—illustrated with code samples, case studies, and discussion of hardware platforms and best practices.
