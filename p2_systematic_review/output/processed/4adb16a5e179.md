## Abstract summary
This review surveys architectures and models for distributed quantum computing (DQC), comparing quantum-classical hybrid systems and fully quantum distributed systems and detailing challenges such as coherence, communication overhead, and error correction. It highlights DQC applications in optimization (logistics and finance), quantum simulation for materials science, and quantum machine learning, and outlines future directions for improving entanglement distribution, hardware, and hybrid integration with classical systems.
