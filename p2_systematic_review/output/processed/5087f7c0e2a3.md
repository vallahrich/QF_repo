## Abstract summary
The paper implements two quantum optimization algorithms (using Qiskit) to model and optimize risk and returns for trade finance portfolios, mapping financial risk/return into a quantum optimization objective. It compares selections from the quantum algorithms against experienced underwriters and a classical optimizer, finding no current performance advantage for quantum methods but also no statistically significant degradation, and concludes the approach is promising as quantum hardware and capabilities improve.
## Methodology
The authors map the financial risk and returns of trade-finance portfolios to a quantum optimization objective (based on a Qiskit tutorial implementation) and compare three solution approaches: a classical exact/minimum-eigenvalue solver, a Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) implementation and a Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) implementation. Financial/performance parameters for candidate counterparties (drawn from company filings and country/sector risk data) were combined into weighted numeric scores (prepared in spreadsheets and imported into Python/Pandas). Those scores were used to derive portfolio returns and covariance inputs and then encoded into the optimization formulation used by the quantum algorithms. All quantum algorithm runs were executed on quantum simulators (to avoid variability from noisy hardware). Results (selected portfolio subsets) were compared against selections made by experienced underwriters and against the classical optimizer; comparisons used overlap/accuracy style metrics and distribution comparisons to assess whether quantum approaches produced materially different or degraded selections. The paper reports that no advantage was observed for the quantum methods on the tested problem instances, but also no statistically significant degradation versus the classical baseline.

**Algorithms used:** Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE), Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA), Classical minimum-eigenvalue / classical optimizer (exact solver)
**Frameworks:** Qiskit, IBM Quantum (service/tools mentioned), Python (Pandas/NumPy/Excel data preprocessing)

**Experimental setup:** Experiments were run on quantum simulators (Qiskit-based simulators were used rather than noisy QPUs) with the quantum algorithms implemented following a Qiskit tutorial. Data preprocessing and score aggregation were performed in Excel and Python/Pandas before encoding into the optimization problem.

**Dataset:** Company-level performance and risk metrics aggregated from public sources (annual reports, regulatory filings such as 10-Ks, management/board information), plus country-level risk/economic/political parameters and sectoral indicators. Metrics were combined via a weighted scoring matrix to produce return estimates and covariances used as inputs for the optimization.
