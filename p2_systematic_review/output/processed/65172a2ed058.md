## Abstract summary
The paper proposes an entanglement-enhanced quantum-inspired optimization (ELSA-GNQTS) that leverages a trend ratio evaluation and sliding-window training to construct short-selling portfolios in G7 markets. Empirical results on 2013–2022 data show the method produces portfolios with higher trend ratios, lower risk and maximum drawdown, and improved profit factors compared with Sharpe-ratio-guided baselines.
## Methodology
The paper proposes an entanglement-enhanced quantum-inspired optimization approach (ELSA-GNQTS) for constructing short-selling portfolios in G7 markets, evaluated using a trend-ratio performance metric. The methodology combines a quantum-inspired tabu-search framework (GNQTS) with (i) a binary portfolio encoding, (ii) a probabilistic beta-matrix representing quantum-like superposition states, (iii) measurement to collapse probabilities into candidate portfolios, (iv) a Quantum-Not gate operation to swap selection probabilities guided by the global-best and local-worst solutions, (v) a probability update rule biased toward global-best components, and (vi) an Entanglement Local Search-Assisted (ELSA) mechanism that explores neighborhood solutions including Hamming-1 moves and entanglement-like multi-bit moves (e.g., coupled add/remove of 2 or more assets). A sliding-window protocol (rebalancing frequencies: year, half-year, quarter, month) and funds-standardization preprocessing are used to maintain training-data freshness and to convert prices into fund-volatility series used by the trend-ratio evaluator. Experiments are carried out for 7 markets (G7), each using the top 30 market-cap stocks with complete 10-year price histories (Jan 2013–Dec 2022). Optimization returns portfolios maximizing trend ratio; results are compared to single-stock selections (best individual by trend ratio) and a Sharpe-ratio-guided baseline across metrics including trend ratio, risk (funds-standardization deviation), maximum drawdown, and profit factor.

**Algorithms used:** Entanglement Local Search-Assisted Global-best guided Quantum-inspired Tabu Search (ELSA-GNQTS), Global-best guided Quantum-inspired Tabu Search (GNQTS), Quantum-inspired Tabu Search (QTS) (referenced), Quantum-Not gate (QN gate) (quantum-inspired operator), Trend Ratio (evaluation metric), Sliding-window rebalancing

**Experimental setup:** Classical (quantum-inspired) experiments on financial price data across 7 markets. No quantum hardware or specific simulator is reported. Optimization run settings: population (individuals) = 10, generations = 10,000, independent experiments = 50, initial fund = 1,000,000,000 (local currency), theta (probability update step) = 0.0002. Best solution taken across 50 independent runs.

**Dataset:** Price and market-cap data for the 30 largest-capitalization stocks (with complete 10-year histories) from each of 7 major indices (G7 markets: FTSE (UK), DJIA (US), TSX (Canada), CAC (France), NIKKEI (Japan), DAX (Germany), MIB (Italy)). Time range: January 2013 to December 2022. Capitalization sources: TradingView and Investing.com. Price data sources: Wharton Research Data Service (WRDS), Yahoo Finance, and Investing.com.
