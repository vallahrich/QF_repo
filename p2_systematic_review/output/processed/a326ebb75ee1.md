## Abstract summary
The paper proposes Quantum Ensemble Optimisation (QEO), a hybrid approach that integrates QAOA and VQE to improve investment portfolio optimization by balancing diversification and risk-adjusted returns. Evaluated on historical stock data (Spotify, Microsoft, Meta), the ensemble method is reported to outperform classical and individual quantum approaches by better exploring the solution space and yielding higher probabilities of sampling optimal portfolio allocations.
## Methodology
The study implements a hybrid 'Quantum Ensemble Optimisation (QEO)' pipeline that integrates the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) and the Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) to produce portfolio allocation recommendations. The workflow begins with investor-provided objectives, constraints and risk tolerance; a portfolio manager component collects relevant stock data which is preprocessed (visualization, return/covariance computation). A classical baseline optimization is performed using a NumPy-based eigen-solver. QAOA and VQE are then run independently to produce candidate portfolio solutions; an ensemble module combines the outputs of QAOA and VQE to generate a final recommended portfolio and to produce probability distributions over possible portfolio combinations. Recommendations are validated by a final 'reality check' comparing suggested purchase prices to historical lower price ranges. Evaluation compares the classical baseline, individual quantum approaches (QAOA, VQE) and the ensemble model on criteria including risk-adjusted return, diversification and probability of sampling the optimal solution.

**Algorithms used:** QAOA, VQE, Quantum Ensemble Optimisation (QEO), NumPy EigenSolver

**Dataset:** Historical equity price data for three companies: Spotify (SPOT), Microsoft (MSFT) and Meta (META), obtained from YahooDataProvider covering the period 2021–2022. The dataset was used to compute returns and a covariance matrix for portfolio optimization.
