## Abstract summary
The paper introduces PO-QA, a scalable framework to systematically explore quantum circuit parameters (rotation blocks, entanglement types, and repetitions) for VQE and QAOA when solving portfolio optimization problems. Using historical market data and an exact eigensolver as a classical benchmark, the study finds QAOA to be more robust and closer to classical solutions while VQE shows high sensitivity to circuit design, and highlights correlations between quantum-architecture choices, risk level, and convergence behavior.
## Methodology
The authors propose PO-QA, a systematic experimental framework to evaluate how quantum-circuit design choices affect Portfolio Optimization (PO) solutions produced by Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) and Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA), using an exact eigensolver as a classical reference. The methodology comprises three main stages: (1) Data preparation — historical market data (periodic means µ and covariance σ) for the selected assets are converted into a quadratic program with linear constraints and mapped to a Hamiltonian whose ground state corresponds to the optimal portfolio; (2) Quantum-circuit exploration — for both VQE and QAOA the authors sweep a discrete design space of circuit parameters (rotation gate type: rx or ry; entanglement gate type: cz or cx; entanglement topology: full, pairwise, circular; and block repetitions/depth: 3 or 5), producing 12 labeled circuit configurations (B–M); (3) Quantum vs classical comparison — for each circuit configuration and for a range of risk factors (0.1 to 0.9) they run the algorithms, record the predicted minimum ground-state energy (and resulting portfolio), and compare it to the classical exact eigensolver solution. Analysis focuses on convergence behavior, sensitivity to circuit parameters, and the proportion of quantum runs that exactly match the classical optimal portfolio. The study uses Qiskit for implementation and evaluates results on real-market historical data (8 assets over a six-month period).

**Algorithms used:** VQE, QAOA, Exact eigensolver (classical)
**Frameworks:** Qiskit

**Dataset:** Historical prices of 8 stocks obtained via Qiskit's Wikipedia DataProvider for the period 2016-07-01 to 2016-12-31 (6 months). Assets: TSLA, AMZN, GOOG, AAPL, FSLR, SPWR, ARRY, ENPH. Inputs to the PO formulation were per-asset periodic mean returns (µ) and the covariance matrix (σ).

**Results summary:** The paper presents PO-QA, a framework for systematically exploring quantum circuit design choices (rotation gates, entanglement block and structure, and repetitions) for portfolio optimization using VQE and QAOA on a real 8-asset dataset (6 months). Empirically, QAOA showed greater robustness to the tested circuit-parameter variations and matched the classical exact eigensolver more frequently than VQE. VQE performance was highly sensitive to these parameters (notably at risk = 0.5), and increasing circuit depth improved VQE results. The experiments also showed a monotonically increasing relationship between risk factor and ground-state energy. The work provides a reproducible experimental base and highlights parameter configurations that merit deeper investigation, while positioning the framework as scalable and useful for further quantum-finance studies.
