## Abstract summary
The paper examines hybrid quantum–classical frameworks for financial decision-making, assessing applications in portfolio management, risk assessment, supply chain finance, and strategic resource allocation. It concludes that quantum-enhanced modules can expand solution-space exploration and improve optimization and sampling efficiency, while highlighting NISQ-era hardware limits, interpretability, governance, and implementation challenges.
## Methodology
This paper is a non‑empirical, review and conceptual analysis that synthesizes existing literature on quantum computing and its implications for financial decision‑making, management science, and strategic planning. The author conducts a narrative literature review and thematic synthesis of theoretical foundations (quantum principles and algorithms), hybrid quantum–classical architectural designs, and emerging application domains (portfolio management, risk assessment, supply chain finance, strategic resource allocation).
