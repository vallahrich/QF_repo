## Abstract summary
The paper investigates applying quantum algorithms (QAOA, Quantum Annealing, VQE) to portfolio optimization, arguing that quantum computation can encode financial optimization problems as Hamiltonians to explore large solution spaces via superposition and entanglement. Through empirical modeling and simulations the study finds that quantum and hybrid quantum–classical approaches can improve risk-adjusted return optimization, efficient frontier computation, and real-time portfolio rebalancing compared with conventional methods.
## Methodology
The paper presents a theoretical investigation into applying quantum algorithms to portfolio optimization.
