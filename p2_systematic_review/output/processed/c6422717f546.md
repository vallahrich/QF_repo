## Abstract summary
The paper proposes a hybrid algorithm that combines Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) with an improved Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) quantum-coded initial population to solve enterprise portfolio optimization problems. Experiments on 20 stocks over 50 trading days show the method yields lower variance and better risk-return tradeoffs than DE, GA and standard PSO, with stable performance as risk levels increase.
## Methodology
The authors propose a hybrid optimization method that combines Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) with a quantum-inspired Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) style quantum population coding to solve a constrained enterprise portfolio optimization problem. They formulate a portfolio objective that integrates return and risk into an asymmetric Return-Risk Criterion (PRC) that penalizes portfolios exceeding an investor's risk tolerance and amplifies benefits when risk is within tolerance. Empirically, they import historical returns for M = 20 assets over n = 50 trading days, compute per-asset average returns and the covariance matrix, then use these statistics as inputs to the hybrid optimizer. The hybrid algorithm represents each particle as a quantum-coded chromosome of dimension M, maintains local and global attractors, computes a mean-best solution, repairs infeasible solutions to satisfy the budget constraint, evaluates fitness using the PRC, and updates particle positions using PSO-inspired quantum update equations (including a potential-well length, mean-optimal term, and contraction/expansion coefficient). The method is compared against standard PSO, a Genetic Algorithm (GA) and Differential Evolution (DE) on the same dataset; outputs include optimal allocation vectors, portfolio return, and realized risk (variance), plus aggregate statistics (max/min/mean/std) across generated portfolios and risk levels.

**Algorithms used:** Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) - quantum-inspired coding, Genetic Algorithm (GA) (baseline comparison), Differential Evolution (DE) (baseline comparison)
**Frameworks:** MatlabR2018b

**Experimental setup:** Computations performed in MatlabR2018b on an Intel(R) Core(TM) i7-4702MQ CPU @ 2.20GHz with 4 GB RAM. Simulation parameters: population size = 20, max iterations = 200, PRC magnification factor (lambda) = 50, investment lower limit = 0.05, investment upper limit = 0.3, quantum chromosome encoding length reported as 10*14.

**Dataset:** Historical yield (return) data for 20 stocks across different sectors from a (unnamed) stock exchange consisting of 50 trading days between 2024-06-24 and 2024-09-01. Per-asset averages and covariance matrix are computed and used as expected returns and risk inputs.
