## Abstract summary
The paper proposes a Quantum-Assisted Artificial Intelligence (QAAI) framework that integrates quantum algorithms (e.g., QSVM, QNN) and variational quantum circuits within hybrid quantum-classical architectures to accelerate large-scale data processing. Experiments on healthcare, finance and cybersecurity datasets report comparable accuracy with conventional AI while reducing training time by 30–40%, and incorporate randomized measurement protocols and encoding strategies to improve noise resilience, though hardware noise and encoding overhead remain limiting factors.
## Methodology
The paper proposes and experimentally evaluates a hybrid Quantum-Assisted Artificial Intelligence (QAAI) framework that combines classical preprocessing and postprocessing with quantum-enhanced learning components. Classical data (from healthcare, finance and cybersecurity domains) are preprocessed (normalization, feature scaling, balancing) and then encoded into quantum states using amplitude or basis encoding. Quantum components used include variational quantum circuits (VQCs) for parameterized learning and optimization, a quantum support vector machine (QSVM) for binary classification (finance experiment) and a quantum neural network (QNN) for multiclass tasks (cybersecurity). Training is performed in a hybrid quantum–classical loop: the quantum circuit evaluates cost or kernel quantities and a classical optimizer updates parameters iteratively to minimize cost. Randomized measurement protocols are integrated to improve measurement-based noise resilience. Experiments compare the QAAI framework against classical baselines (traditional SVMs, deep learning and other ML models) using metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score, and report reductions in training time and improvements in accuracy for the reported domains. Implementation used Python with Qiskit and Pennylane, targeting IBM Quantum hardware and simulators, and classical computation on an AMD Ryzen 7 5800H with 16 GB RAM.

**Algorithms used:** Quantum Support Vector Machine (QSVM), Quantum Neural Network (QNN), Variational Quantum Circuits (VQCs), Randomized Measurement Protocols, Hybrid Quantum–Classical Optimization
**Frameworks:** IBM Quantum Experience, Pennylane, Qiskit, Python

**Experimental setup:** Quantum circuits and simulations were developed with Pennylane and Qiskit and executed against IBM Quantum Experience (simulators and IBM superconducting-qubit hardware). Classical preprocessing and hybrid optimization ran on a workstation with an AMD Ryzen 7 5800H CPU and 16 GB RAM. Implementation language: Python.

**Dataset:** Financial experiment: stock market trend and high-frequency trading data (real-time trading patterns) encoded with amplitude encoding. No public dataset name or source, nor dataset size, is specified in the paper.
