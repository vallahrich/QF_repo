## Abstract summary
The paper proposes a quantum virtualization framework that enables multi-tenant quantum virtual machines (qVMs) to provide scalable, flexible, and secure access to scarce quantum hardware for FinTech workloads. It introduces layered abstraction, dynamic qubit allocation, and workload-adaptive circuit scheduling, and presents benchmarks showing up to 40% improved hardware utilization, reduced queuing latency, and tenant isolation without compromising circuit fidelity, positioning qVMs as an enabler for cost-efficient quantum-as-a-service in financial services.
