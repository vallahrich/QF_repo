## Abstract summary
The paper proposes a hybrid quantum-classical deep learning (HQDL) approach for financial market trend prediction that integrates quantum neural networks (QNNs) and variational quantum circuits (VQCs) with classical CNN and LSTM components. Using quantum feature encoding and hybrid optimization on historical prices, technical indicators and macroeconomic data, the authors report improved accuracy, robustness in volatile markets, reduced training/inference time and lower energy consumption compared to classical deep models.
## Methodology
The authors propose a hybrid quantum-classical deep learning (HQDL) methodology for financial market trend prediction. The workflow begins with collecting multi-source financial data (historical prices, volumes, technical indicators, macroeconomic factors, news and social media sentiment). Data are cleaned, normalized, missing values imputed via linear interpolation, and feature-engineered (moving averages, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, etc.). Selected classical features are mapped into quantum states using quantum feature encodings (angle, amplitude, or basis encoding) to lift inputs into a higher-dimensional Hilbert space. The model architecture is hybrid: parameterized Variational Quantum Circuits (VQCs) / Quantum Neural Networks (QNNs) are used for quantum-enhanced feature processing and are integrated with classical deep modules (CNNs for spatial/feature extraction and LSTMs for temporal modeling). A hybrid optimization strategy is employed where classical optimizers (Adam or gradient-based methods) update both classical and parametrized quantum circuit parameters; quantum gradient descent variants are also considered. Training is hybrid: quantum components can be executed on simulators or real QPUs while classical components run on GPUs. Hyperparameters (learning rate, batch size, number of quantum layers) are tuned via grid or Bayesian search; regularization methods such as dropout and early stopping are applied to control overfitting. Performance is benchmarked against classical baselines (LSTM, CNN-LSTM, Transformer) using regression and trading-oriented metrics, and experiments are performed on quantum simulators/backends (PennyLane, Qiskit, and provider backends like IBM Quantum, Google Quantum AI, Rigetti) with classical DL implemented in TensorFlow/PyTorch.

**Algorithms used:** Quantum Neural Networks (QNN), Variational Quantum Circuits (VQC), Quantum Feature Encoding (QFE) (angle/amplitude/basis encoding), Hybrid quantum-classical optimization, Adam optimizer, Quantum gradient descent (investigated), Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM), Transformer (used as baseline)
**Frameworks:** PennyLane, Qiskit, Rigetti Forest SDK, IBM Quantum (backends), Google Quantum AI (backends), TensorFlow, PyTorch

**Experimental setup:** Quantum portions were executed on quantum simulators and provider backends (IBM Quantum, Google Quantum AI, Rigetti) and implemented in PennyLane and Qiskit; classical deep-learning modules were implemented in TensorFlow and PyTorch and trained on GPUs. Different quantum backend configurations (to reflect realistic microwave-channel hardware limitations) were explored in experiments.

**Dataset:** High-frequency financial data aggregated from multiple markets (global stock exchanges, cryptocurrency markets, and Forex). Dataset includes historical price series, trading volume, technical indicators, market sentiment (news and social media), and macroeconomic factors (interest rates, inflation, GDP). The dataset spans multiple years to capture short- and long-term trends.
