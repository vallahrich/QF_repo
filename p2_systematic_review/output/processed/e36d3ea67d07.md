## Abstract summary
The paper introduces QPDD, a hybrid quantum-classical probabilistic database framework that leverages quantum superposition, entanglement, Quantum Bayesian Networks (QBNs), and quantum annealing to accelerate probabilistic inference and solve NP-hard optimization for real-time decision support under high-dimensional uncertainty. Experimental results claim large improvements over classical probabilistic databases (e.g., up to ~80% faster queries, ~15% higher inference accuracy, and ~65% faster optimization) and demonstrate applicability to domains such as healthcare, finance, and smart infrastructure while noting hardware and error-correction challenges for real-world deployment.
## Methodology
The paper proposes a conceptual hybrid quantum-classical framework (QPDD) that integrates a quantum processor with classical probabilistic databases to accelerate probabilistic inference and NP-hard optimization for real-time decision support. Method components described include encoding uncertain variables into qubits using quantum superposition for parallel evaluation of scenarios, using quantum entanglement to represent and detect correlations among dependent variables, adopting Quantum Bayesian Networks (QBNs) to perform probabilistic inference via quantum gates, and employing a Quantum Annealing–based Optimization Engine to solve combinatorial decision problems. The authors report a high-level experimental evaluation in which the hybrid system is benchmarked against classical probabilistic databases and classical Bayesian networks on metrics such as query execution time, prediction accuracy, optimization time, and scalability (max dataset size / processing efficiency). The paper, however, does not provide low-level implementation details (no specific quantum algorithms/parametrizations, dataset sources, hardware/QPU models, simulator names, or code), so the methodology is presented at an architectural and algorithmic-concept level rather than as a fully specified empirical protocol.

**Algorithms used:** Quantum Annealing (Quantum Annealing–based Optimization Engine), Quantum Bayesian Networks (QBNs), Quantum Markov Models, Quantum Monte Carlo simulations
