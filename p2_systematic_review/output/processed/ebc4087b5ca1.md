## Abstract summary
This open-access edited volume provides an interdisciplinary, system-level treatment of securing AI/ML pipelines in mortgage and banking against quantum-era threats. It synthesizes research and practice on post-quantum cryptography, federated and privacy-preserving learning, secure training, hybrid authentication, adversarial resilience, explainable AI, performance impacts, and formal validation, and offers practical guidance for transitioning financial systems to quantum-resilient architectures while meeting regulatory and governance requirements.

Practical adoption pathways emphasize crypto-agility and hybrid classical+PQC strategies to balance immediate operational needs with future-proofing. Federated learning, PQ-secure training/inference, secure MPC, homomorphic encryption, and PQ-aware authentication are highlighted as key building blocks, but all impose significant performance, integration, and governance challenges. Formal validation, explainability in encrypted/secure contexts, and domain-specific threat models are underdeveloped and recommended priorities. Overall, while PQC and associated defenses are technically feasible and increasingly standardized, realizing them in large-scale, latency-sensitive financial AI requires architectural redesign, hardware acceleration, regulatory alignment, and interdisciplinary work.
