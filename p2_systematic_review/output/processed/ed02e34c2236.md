## Abstract summary
This review provides an accessible introduction to quantum computing for IT professionals, covering foundational concepts (qubits, superposition, entanglement), quantum gates and key algorithms (Shor, Grover, VQE), and major hardware approaches (superconducting, trapped ions, photonic). It discusses practical implications for cryptography and the emergence of post-quantum standards, highlights near-term application areas such as chemistry, optimization, and finance, and assesses market and development timelines toward practical quantum advantage.
## Methodology
This paper is a non-empirical, narrative review / introductory survey intended for IT professionals and developers.
