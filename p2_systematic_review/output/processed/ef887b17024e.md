## Abstract summary
This review surveys potential applications of quantum computing across domains including machine learning, optimization, simulation of molecules and materials, cybersecurity (e.g., QKD), finance, supply chain, energy and climate modelling. The authors note that while quantum computing is still in early stages, it promises significant computational advantages for complex problems and calls for further research and development to realize practical industry impact.
## Methodology
This paper is a narrative literature review (review-article) that surveys and synthesizes existing research, projects and industry initiatives on quantum computing and its potential applications across multiple domains (cybersecurity, pharmaceuticals, finance, supply chain, energy, machine learning, climate modelling, materials, etc.). The authors draw on a broad set of cited references (journal articles, conference papers, technical reports, and industry projects) to describe algorithms, example applications, and illustrative projects or startups. The article does not report any original empirical experiments, does not describe a formal systematic search strategy (databases searched, date ranges, search terms), and does not provide explicit inclusion/exclusion criteria, quality assessment, or PRISMA-style methodology. Instead it provides a topical synthesis and examples referenced in the literature and public projects to highlight potential use-cases and algorithmic approaches.
