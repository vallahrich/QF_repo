## Abstract summary
This review surveys quantum algorithms for optimization — including QAOA, VQE, Grover's algorithm, quantum annealing and adiabatic computing — and discusses their implementation on NISQ devices and hybrid quantum-classical workflows. It highlights applications in logistics, finance and machine learning, and summarizes key challenges such as noise, scalability, circuit depth and parameter optimization while noting that hybrid approaches and hardware improvements may enable near-term benefits.
## Methodology
This paper is a narrative literature review and conceptual analysis of quantum algorithms applied to optimization problems, with emphasis on applications relevant to finance and logistics. The authors surveyed recent theoretical and experimental work (as cited) on hybrid quantum-classical and NISQ-era approaches, identified and described key algorithms (QAOA, VQE, Grover, quantum annealing, adiabatic quantum computing, and related variational methods), and synthesized findings on strengths, challenges, and application domains. The review organizes prior work into thematic sections (literature review, algorithm descriptions, comparative tables and figures), extracts reported advantages and limitations (e.g., noise, decoherence, barren plateaus, scalability), and produces qualitative comparisons and scored assessments of algorithm performance across problem types (optimization, graph problems, search, quantum chemistry). The authors also discuss hardware trends, hybrid strategies, and error mitigation approaches based on existing studies, and cite representative experimental and applied papers (e.g., D-Wave annealing applications, QAOA in logistics and finance). No original empirical experiments, datasets, or new simulation results are reported; instead the methodology is synthesis and critical analysis of prior published work to highlight opportunities and bottlenecks for quantum optimization in practice.
