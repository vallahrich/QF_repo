## Abstract summary
This review surveys quantum machine learning (QML) techniques that combine quantum computing and classical machine learning, covering quantum data encoding, variational algorithms (e.g., VQE, QAOA), quantum neural networks, quantum SVMs, QBMs and QGANs. It highlights potential quantum speedups for optimization and classification, applications in drug discovery, finance and cryptography, and discusses current hardware limitations, noise challenges, and the role of hybrid quantum-classical approaches.
## Methodology
The paper describes a hybrid quantum-classical methodology typical of Variational Quantum Algorithms (VQAs). Classical data are first encoded into quantum states using encoding schemes (e.g., amplitude, basis, and angle encoding) via quantum operators (the paper refers generically to operators V^1, V^2, V^3). Parameterized quantum circuits (unitaries U^(Φ1), U^(Φ2), U^(Φ3) in the text) are then applied to process these quantum-encoded features and extract quantum-enhanced representations. Measurements are performed on the output states and multiple samples are taken to estimate expectation values and mitigate probabilistic measurement noise. Extracted classical observables are fed into classical models (for example a neural network or SVM) which compute a cost function. Classical optimization routines (gradient descent, stochastic gradient descent, Adam and other classical optimizers are mentioned) are used to iteratively update the parameters of the quantum circuit to minimize the cost. The workflow emphasizes sampling/averaging to estimate measurement outcomes, iterative hybrid optimization loops, and the use of quantum circuits for feature mapping within broader pipelines for QNNs, QSVMs, QBMs and related QML models. The methodology is presented at a conceptual level rather than as an experimental protocol.

**Algorithms used:** Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE), Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA), Quantum Neural Networks (QNNs) / Parameterized Quantum Circuits, Quantum Support Vector Machines (QSVMs) / Quantum Kernel Methods, Quantum Boltzmann Machines (QBMs), Quantum Generative Adversarial Networks (QGANs), Quantum Reinforcement Learning (QRL)

**Dataset:** Financial time-series data (stock market and risk assessment datasets) are mentioned as an application domain; no specific financial dataset or empirical experiment is reported.
