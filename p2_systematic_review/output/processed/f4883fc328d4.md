## Abstract summary
The paper proposes an error-resilient hybrid quantum-classical framework that integrates variational quantum circuits (VQE/QAOA), adaptive circuit depth, and noise mitigation techniques such as Zero Noise Extrapolation and dynamical decoupling to address real-world optimization problems in finance and supply chain. Experiments on real datasets using IBM, Google, and Rigetti hardware and simulations demonstrate improved convergence, lower error (MAPE), enhanced noise robustness, and computational speedups compared to classical baselines.
## Methodology
The study develops a hybrid quantum-classical framework for real-world optimization in finance and supply-chain domains. Problems are encoded as Hamiltonians (sum of Pauli terms) and mapped to either QUBO or domain-specific Hamiltonians (e.g., portfolio H = -sum r_i z_i + sum q_ij z_i z_j). Parameterized quantum circuits (ansatz) composed of layers of single-qubit RY rotations and CNOT entangling gates are used to prepare trial states U(θ). Two variational algorithms—VQE and QAOA—are employed on NISQ devices/simulators: the quantum component evaluates expectation values E(θ)=⟨ψ(θ)|H|ψ(θ)⟩ while a classical optimizer iteratively updates θ to minimize the cost. Noise resilience techniques are integrated: Zero Noise Extrapolation (ZNE) for extrapolating to the zero-noise limit, dynamical decoupling pulse sequences, and error-aware optimizers that adapt learning rates based on noise profiles. The framework features adaptive circuit-depth selection (iteratively increasing depth balanced against hardware noise), multi-objective optimization capability via Pareto analyses, and domain-specific customization (portfolio and supply-chain encodings). Implementation used Qiskit and Cirq, with experiments run on IBM Quantum Experience, Google Sycamore, a Rigetti processor and classical simulators. Performance was evaluated using convergence behavior, Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE), fidelity under noise, and computational time versus classical baselines (simulated annealing, genetic algorithms).

**Algorithms used:** VQE, QAOA
**Frameworks:** Qiskit, Cirq

**Experimental setup:** Experiments used real-world datasets (historical S&P 500 for portfolio optimization; UCI Logistics dataset for supply chain). Quantum runs were performed on IBM Quantum Experience, Google Sycamore, and a Rigetti quantum processor, with additional classical simulations for comparison. Classical baselines included simulated annealing (finance) and genetic algorithms (supply chain). Performance was evaluated across varying problem sizes (portfolio sizes 10, 50, 100 assets; supply-chain nodes 10, 50, 100).

**Dataset:** Historical S&P 500 stock data (for portfolio optimization) and UCI Logistics dataset (for supply-chain demand/transportation optimization).
