## Instructions

You are a research assistant compressing inductive codes into a structured analytical memo for a systematic literature review on quantum computing in financial services.

## Inputs

### Paper metadata (from Phase 2 classification)
{p2_frontmatter}

### Inductive codes (from Pass A1 — verified codes only)
{a1_codes}

### Quantitative extraction (if available, otherwise empty)
{quantitative_extraction}

### Triangulation verdict (if available, otherwise "not_triangulated")
{triangulation_verdict}

## Task

Synthesise the A1 codes into a structured analytical memo. Organise the codes into the 9 reporting dimensions below. Every dimension's text MUST cite specific code IDs from the A1 input.

## 9 Reporting Dimensions

1. **problem_framing** — What finance problem does the paper address and how is it formulated for quantum computation?
2. **quantum_method** — Which quantum algorithm or approach is used? Implementation details?
3. **key_results** — What are the main reported results and metrics?
4. **classical_comparison** — How does the approach compare to classical methods (if reported)?
5. **stated_limitations** — What limitations does the paper acknowledge?
6. **future_work** — What future work directions are identified?
7. **novelty_claim** — What does the paper claim as its contribution?
8. **methodological_notes** — Observations about experimental rigour, reproducibility, dataset quality.
9. **miscellaneous** — Codes that do not fit any of the above dimensions. Do NOT force-fit codes — if a code doesn't belong in dimensions 1-8, place it here.

## Output Format

Return a JSON object:

```json
{{
  "paper_id": "{paper_id}",
  "dimension_1_problem_framing": {{
    "text": "<1-3 sentence summary>",
    "support_codes": ["C-001", "C-003"],
    "support_type": "direct_quote"
  }},
  "dimension_2_quantum_method": {{
    "text": "<1-3 sentence summary>",
    "support_codes": ["C-005", "C-007"],
    "support_type": "author_claim"
  }},
  ...
  "dimension_9_miscellaneous": {{
    "text": "<summary of codes that don't fit elsewhere, or 'none'>",
    "support_codes": [],
    "support_type": "direct_quote"
  }}
}}
```

`support_type` must be one of:
- `direct_quote` — the summary is directly supported by verbatim quotes in the cited codes
- `author_claim` — the summary reflects the author's stated claims
- `model_inference` — the summary includes interpretation not directly stated by the author (FLAG for researcher review)

RULES:
1. Every dimension MUST have at least one support_code (except dimension_9 if empty).
2. Every code ID you cite MUST exist in the A1 input. Do NOT invent code IDs.
3. If a dimension has no relevant codes, write "Not addressed in this paper" and leave support_codes empty.
4. Preserve author hedging — if the codes use cautious language, the memo must too.
5. LIMITATIONS ARE CRITICAL — dimension_5 (stated_limitations) must capture EVERY limitation the paper acknowledges. Check the paper's discussion, conclusion, and any "limitations" subsection. Do not omit caveats.
6. ATTRIBUTION — only attribute results to THIS paper. If the paper cites someone else's result, say "the authors cite [X] who found..." not "the paper found..."
7. BALANCE — draw from ALL sections of the paper, not just abstract and introduction. Results and discussion sections carry the most important content.

Output ONLY the JSON object. No markdown wrapping.
