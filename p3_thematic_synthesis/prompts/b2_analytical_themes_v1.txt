## Instructions

You are a research assistant performing **Stage 3 of Thomas & Harden (2008) thematic synthesis**: merging batch-level descriptive themes and developing them into **analytical themes** for a systematic literature review on quantum computing in financial services.

The descriptive themes were produced by B1 across one or more batches of this silo. Your task has two parts:
1. **Merge**: consolidate overlapping/duplicate descriptive themes across batches into a unified silo-level set.
2. **Interpret**: for each merged descriptive theme (or cluster), produce an analytical theme — a higher-order interpretation that goes beyond description to explanation, implication, and contestation.

Analytical themes answer *"What does this pattern MEAN? Why does it exist? What does it imply for the field?"*

## Inputs

### Silo context
Silo: {silo_name} ({silo_code})
Papers in scope: {silo_paper_count}
Analytical-theme cap for this silo: {max_analytical_themes}  (between 4 and 12, size-scaled)

### Descriptive themes from all B1 batches
{all_descriptive_themes}

## Output Format

Return a JSON object. ONLY the JSON object — no markdown, no prose.

```json
{{
  "silo": "{silo_code}",
  "descriptive_themes": [
    {{
      "theme_id": "DT-{silo_code}-001",
      "theme_label": "<merged descriptive theme name>",
      "description": "<merged 2-3 sentence description>",
      "supporting_papers": ["<paper_ids, union of merged B1 themes>"],
      "support_code_ids": {{
        "<paper_id>": ["<code_ids>"]
      }},
      "merged_from": ["DT-{silo_code}-B01-003", "DT-{silo_code}-B02-001"]
    }}
  ],
  "analytical_themes": [
    {{
      "theme_id": "AT-{silo_code}-001",
      "theme_label": "<analytical theme name — interpretive, not descriptive>",
      "interpretation": "<2-4 sentences: the higher-order interpretation. Explains WHY the pattern exists or what it MEANS.>",
      "grounded_in": ["DT-{silo_code}-001", "DT-{silo_code}-003"],
      "supporting_papers": ["<union of papers from grounded descriptive themes>"],
      "counter_evidence": [
        {{
          "paper_id": "<paper_id that appears in silo scope>",
          "reason": "<1-2 sentences explaining what this paper shows that contests or qualifies the theme>",
          "evidence_type": "opposing_claim"
        }}
      ],
      "no_counter_evidence_reason": null,
      "implication": "<1-2 sentences: what this means for the field's trajectory / for practice / for the P4 experiment selection>"
    }}
  ]
}}
```

## Hard rules

1. **Theme IDs**:
   - Merged descriptive themes use `DT-{silo_code}-NNN` (no batch suffix — these are silo-level post-merge).
   - Analytical themes use `AT-{silo_code}-NNN`.
2. **Merged provenance**: every merged descriptive theme's `merged_from` list must reference real B1 theme IDs from the input; its `supporting_papers` is the union of the merged inputs; its `support_code_ids` must aggregate the code IDs.
3. **Grounding**: every analytical theme's `grounded_in` must reference at least one merged descriptive theme from your own output.
4. **Supporting papers (analytical)**: the analytical theme's `supporting_papers` must be a subset of the union of papers from its `grounded_in` descriptive themes.
5. **Counter-evidence**: every analytical theme MUST have either (a) at least one entry in `counter_evidence` with a paper_id that is in the silo's scope and a concrete reason, OR (b) a non-null `no_counter_evidence_reason` explaining why the theme is uncontested (e.g., "unanimous finding across all X papers in the silo"). You may NOT leave both empty. Do not invent paper IDs — every cited paper_id must be one of the silo's papers. `evidence_type` is one of: `opposing_claim`, `boundary_condition`, `null_result`, `methodological_critique`.
6. **Analytical theme count**: produce between 4 and {max_analytical_themes} analytical themes. More than {max_analytical_themes} indicates insufficient abstraction. Fewer than 4 indicates over-merging (unless the silo genuinely has few distinct patterns — justify in interpretation).
7. **Add interpretation**: an analytical theme MUST add something beyond the descriptive theme(s) it is grounded in. Do NOT simply rename a descriptive theme. If you cannot find a genuine interpretation, omit the theme.
8. **Anti-anchoring**: do NOT use Phase 1 taxonomy codes (PD-01..PD-10, SA-01..SA-11) or their labels as theme names. Themes must emerge from the evidence.
9. **Output JSON only** — no markdown fences, no commentary, no reasoning traces.
