## Task

Verify this analytical memo against the source paper. Assume the memo is ACCEPTABLE unless you find strong evidence of material misrepresentation.

Compression is normal and expected — a memo that omits details, condenses phrasing, or simplifies explanations is NOT erroneous. Only flag problems where the memo would lead a researcher to an INCORRECT conclusion about the paper.

## Verification Process

1. Identify the 3-5 most important claims in the memo.
2. For each, locate the corresponding passage in the source paper.
3. Check: does the memo fairly represent what the paper says?
4. Only flag if ALL three conditions are met:
   - **Mismatch**: the memo claim is contradicted by, or materially less qualified than, the paper
   - **Consequence**: the mismatch changes the paper's main contribution, evidence strength, scope, validity, or attribution
   - **Centrality**: a reader would likely retain the wrong takeaway from this claim

If any condition fails, do NOT flag it.

## Problem Types

1. CLAIM_STRENGTH_DISTORTION — memo presents a hedged/preliminary/conditional finding as established or general. The author's qualification must be substantive, not cosmetic.
2. SCOPE_SHIFT — memo broadens a result beyond its stated conditions (specific dataset/hardware/problem size → general claim).
3. WRONG_ATTRIBUTION — memo attributes a specific empirical result to this paper when the paper is citing another group's work.
4. NUMERIC_DISTORTION — memo states a wrong number, percentage, metric, or scale that the paper reports differently.
5. OMITTED_VALIDITY_QUALIFIER — memo omits a limitation that the authors explicitly link to their core claims, and the omission makes those claims appear more reliable than the authors intended.

Omission of secondary shortcomings, future work suggestions, or tangential caveats is NOT a problem.

## Output

IMPORTANT: Use EXACTLY this JSON schema. Do NOT use "problems_found", "problem_count", "problems", or "severity" — those are from an old format.

Example of a clean result:
{"paper_id": "abc123", "verdict": "clean", "issues": []}

Example with one material issue:
{"paper_id": "abc123", "verdict": "material_issue", "issues": [{"type": "CLAIM_STRENGTH_DISTORTION", "memo_span": "the paper demonstrates quantum speedup", "source_span": "our preliminary simulation suggests a potential speedup", "source_location": "Results section, paragraph 3", "materiality": "Changes finding from tentative to established", "explanation": "The memo drops 'preliminary' and 'suggests potential', presenting a hedged result as demonstrated."}]}

Default to "clean". Most well-constructed memos should pass verification.

Return valid JSON only.
