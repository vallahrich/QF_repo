## Silo Overlay Instructions

You are creating a silo-specific interpretation of a paper that has already been coded and summarised centrally. Your job is to highlight which aspects are most relevant to the **{silo_name}** problem domain.

## Central A2 Memo (9-dimension summary)

{central_memo}

## A1 Codes (verified only)

{a1_codes}

## Silo Context

**Silo**: {silo_name}
**Description**: {silo_description}

## Task

Produce a silo-specific overlay that identifies which codes and dimensions are most relevant to the {silo_name} domain. This overlay supplements (does not replace) the central memo.

CRITICAL RULES:
1. EVIDENCE-LINKED — every claim must reference specific A1 code_ids from the list above. Do NOT introduce claims that aren't supported by existing codes.
2. CLOSED-WORLD — use ONLY information from the central memo and A1 codes. Do NOT add external knowledge.
3. SILO FOCUS — explain why specific findings matter for {silo_name}, not for finance generally.
4. BREVITY — 3-5 key points, not a full rewrite of the memo.

## Output Format

Return a JSON object:

{{"paper_id": "{paper_id}", "silo": "{silo_name}", "silo_relevance": "1-2 sentence summary of why this paper matters for {silo_name}", "key_points": [{{"point": "Short description of a silo-relevant finding", "support_codes": ["C-001", "C-015"], "dimension_source": "dimension_3", "silo_note": "Why this matters specifically for {silo_name}"}}, ...], "primary_codes": ["C-001", "C-015", "C-023"], "silo_relevance_score": "high|medium|low"}}

Produce 3-5 key_points. List all primary code_ids relevant to this silo in primary_codes. You MUST return valid JSON only.
