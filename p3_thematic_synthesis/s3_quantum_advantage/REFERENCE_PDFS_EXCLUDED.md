# Reference PDFs — Excluded from submission

Each `s3_quantum_advantage/<framework>/` folder originally contains a **reference PDF** of the source paper that defines the assessment framework (Rønnow et al., Beverland et al., Dalzell et al., Hoefler et al., Babbush et al., Chakrabarti et al., Stilck França et al.). These PDFs are excluded from this submission repository due to publisher copyright.

| Framework folder | Excluded PDF | Citation |
|------------------|--------------|----------|
| `babbush_2021/` | `paper.pdf` | Babbush et al. (2021), *Focus beyond quadratic speedups for error-corrected quantum advantage* — arXiv:2011.04149 |
| `beverland_2022/` | `2211.07629v1.pdf` | Beverland et al. (2022), *Assessing requirements to scale to practical quantum advantage* — arXiv:2211.07629 |
| `chakrabarti_2021/` | `paper.pdf`, `chakrabarti_qaoa_2025.pdf` | Chakrabarti et al. (2021), *A threshold for quantum advantage in derivative pricing* — arXiv:2012.03819 |
| `dalzell_2023/` | `paper.pdf` | Dalzell et al. (2023), *Quantum algorithms: a survey of applications and end-to-end complexities* — arXiv:2310.03011 |
| `hoefler_assessment/` | `3571725.pdf` | Hoefler, Häner, Troyer (2023), *Disentangling hype from practicality: on realistically achieving quantum advantage* — Comm. ACM 66(5) |
| `ronnow/` | `1401.2910v1.pdf` | Rønnow et al. (2014), *Defining and detecting quantum speedup* — Science 345(6195) |
| `stilck_franca_2021/` | `2009.05532v1.pdf` | Stilck França & García-Patrón (2021), *Limitations of optimization algorithms on noisy quantum devices* — Nature Physics 17 |

All canonical citation keys for these works appear in `manuscript/07_Bibliography/references.bib` and resolve via the standard literature databases (arXiv, Communications of the ACM, Science, Nature Physics).

The **assessment code** that operationalises each framework (`assess_*.py`) ships in full inside each folder and is the actual evidence for Phase 3 quantum-advantage triangulation. The PDFs are reference material only.

See the project root [`FAIR_USE.md`](../../FAIR_USE.md) for the rationale on excluded source materials.
