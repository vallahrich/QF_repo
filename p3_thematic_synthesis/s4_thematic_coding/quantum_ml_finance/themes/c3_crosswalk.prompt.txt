## Relationship definitions

Pick **exactly one** per crosswalk entry:

- **confirms** — the prior review states essentially the same claim as the theme's interpretation. The theme re-observes something the review has already surfaced, supporting robustness across syntheses.
- **extends** — the prior review touches the claim but this theme adds specificity (a new financial domain, a sharper causal mechanism, a quantitative layer, or a newer evidence base). Use when your theme goes beyond what the review says.
- **contradicts** — the prior review asserts the opposite claim, or its reported evidence points against this theme's interpretation. Use where there is a genuine disagreement that the thesis must engage.
- **complements** — the prior review addresses a related aspect without direct overlap. Use sparingly; a chapter full of `complements` is weak literature positioning.
