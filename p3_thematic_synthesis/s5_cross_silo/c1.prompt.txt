## Hard rules

1. **Silo grounding**: every meta-theme must cite `silos_grounded_in` with **at least 3 distinct silos**. Meta-themes grounded in <3 silos are not cross-silo; they belong in the silo chapters.
2. **Theme grounding**: `grounded_in_themes` must reference actual `AT-XX-NNN` IDs present in the input. Do not invent theme IDs.
3. **Non-obviousness**: a meta-theme must pass the *"so what?"* test. Rejected examples:
   - "Most silos use NISQ hardware" — trivially true; no interpretive content.
   - "The literature claims quantum advantage" — descriptive, not interpretive.
   Accepted examples (illustrative):
   - "Speedup claims decouple from I/O overhead across all speedup-bearing silos, suggesting a shared methodological blind spot in how 'advantage' is operationalized."
   - "Hybridization trajectories diverge sharply by silo maturity: QML collapses into pragmatic hybrid ML, while optimization silos retain aspirational pure-quantum goals."
4. **Divergence engaged**: every meta-theme must have non-trivial `divergence_notes`. If you cannot identify a divergent silo, state this explicitly and justify ("no divergence because the pattern is universal — unanimous across all 8 silos, see AT-XX-NNN in each").
