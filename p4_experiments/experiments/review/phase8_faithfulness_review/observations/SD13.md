### Concerns raised by Vincent

- Label drift: instance.json and circuit.py register this artifact as 'SD14' rather than 'SD13'; downstream registry lookups keyed on label_id may miss this experiment.
- Algorithm proxy is a plain RealAmplitudes ansatz that does not exercise any LCHS/QSVT/OAA/block-encoding primitives the paper centers on; resource estimates derived from it will systematically under-represent the paper's true gate/depth profile.
- Paper exp_2 is a wave-equation material-design problem in derivative_pricing silo; classification as 'derivative-pricing' is loose (the Black-Scholes companion experiment is the derivative-pricing one).
- n_qubits=6 omits the design-variable register entirely (paper: M*m=6 design qubits plus 6 space-plus-ancilla = 12), so the proxy collapses two distinct registers.

### Recommendations from Vincent

- Rename label_id in instance.json (label, instance_id) and in circuit.py (@register label, description, function/circuit names, accounting label) from 'SD14' to 'SD13' to align with the canonical cohort identifier.
- Optionally raise n_qubits to 12 (within the tractability cap) so the proxy at least matches the paper's reported total qubit count for exp_2.
- Consider adding a comment in circuit.py noting that the paper's true algorithm is QHD+LCHS+QSVT (not a variational ansatz) so future readers do not mistake the proxy for a faithful implementation.
