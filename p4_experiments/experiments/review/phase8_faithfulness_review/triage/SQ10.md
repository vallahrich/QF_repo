For the mixed-state kernel-PCA branch, the paper defines a mixed-state analogue of the pure-state kernel-PCA proximity measure and introduces a modified swap-test-style procedure to estimate the required mixed-state inner-product analogue. This is a quantum-kernel / overlap-estimation algorithm, not a variational ansatz algorithm.

    The implemented circuit described in Vincent's review is a generic RealAmplitudes ansatz followed by its inverse and then measurement. That circuit has no mixed-state kernel construction, no Kraus-operator controlled operation, no modified swap-test ancilla for estimating mixed-state overlaps, no density-matrix/fidelity measurement path, and no proximity-measure computation. The compute-uncompute structure is therefore structurally trivial relative to the paper's algorithm.

    The broad cohort family 'other-gate-based' is acceptable only as a catch-all, but it does not make the implementation family-faithful. The circuit registry tag 'quantum-svm' is also misleading for SQ10 because exp_3 is the mixed-state kernel-PCA branch, not the one-class-SVM branch.
