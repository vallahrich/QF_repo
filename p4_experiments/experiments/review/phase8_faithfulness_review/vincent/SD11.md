## Verdict summary

5-qubit RealAmplitudes generator + canonical QAE faithfully proxies the paper's hybrid qWGAN + IQAE option-pricing pipeline at the paper's stated scale.

## Checks

### ✅ Family-faithful

Paper is hybrid quantum GAN (variational quantum generator + classical discriminator) feeding IQAE for option pricing; circuit uses qgan_qae template (generator + controlled Grover^{2^k} + inverse QFT), matching quantum-ml + amplitude-estimation families.

### ✅ Scale-faithful

Paper reports 5 generator qubits and 2 IQAE ancillas; circuit uses n_qubits=5 generator and num_eval_qubits=3 for canonical QAE (within factor-2 of paper m=2; canonical QAE substituted for IQAE is an explicit template choice).

- `v2_n_qubits`: `5`
- `circuit_n_qubits`: `5`

### ✅ Structurally non-trivial

Bare = parameterised RealAmplitudes generator (reps=2) with measurement; Full = generator state-prep + controlled Grover powers + inverse QFT, i.e. the canonical QAE structure expected for an amplitude-estimation oracle.

### ✅ Metadata-consistent

Cohort label_id SD11 corresponds to legacy label 'SD1' used inside circuit.py / instance.json (paper_id af45ea1743f6 matches across cohort, instance, v2 extraction); algorithm_family tag 'quantum-ml' matches cohort and circuit registration.

## Concerns

- Circuit substitutes canonical QAE (with num_eval_qubits=3) for the paper's IQAE (m=2 ancillas); acceptable as a resource-accounting proxy but is a methodology-level simplification.
- Discriminator and Wasserstein training are not implemented (generator is an untrained RealAmplitudes ansatz used as a state-prep proxy), as documented in the cohort notes.
- Internal label string 'SD1' inside circuit.py / instance.json differs from canonical label_id 'SD11'; cosmetic only, but worth normalising.

## Recommendations

- Optionally swap canonical QAE for an IQAE template variant to better mirror the paper's stated estimator and ancilla count (m=2).
- Normalise the legacy 'SD1' label string inside instance.json and circuit registration to 'SD11' for consistency with the canonical cohort id.
