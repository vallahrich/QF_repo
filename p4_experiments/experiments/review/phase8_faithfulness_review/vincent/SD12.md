## Verdict summary

Intentional generic RealAmplitudes template proxy for a QHD/block-encoding/QSVT paper; family bucket and scale are within preregistered proxy policy, but circuit/instance metadata mislabels this entry as 'SD11' instead of 'SD12'.

## Checks

### ✅ Family-faithful

Paper algorithm is QHD with explicit block-encoding (LCU+QSVT+OAA) over an LCHS forward-simulation oracle; cohort places it in 'other-gate-based' and the circuit is the explicitly preregistered ansatz_stretch template proxy. Family bucket matches cohort tag, and the implementation does not claim to reproduce the paper's algorithm.

### ✅ Scale-faithful

Paper reports 19 qubits for the Black-Scholes instance; circuit uses 6 qubits, which is below the documented 12-qubit tractability cap for template proxies, so it is acceptable under the methodology even though 19/6 > 2.

- `v2_n_qubits`: `19`
- `circuit_n_qubits`: `6`

### ✅ Structurally non-trivial

RealAmplitudes(reps=3, linear entanglement) ansatz composed with its inverse (compute-uncompute) and measured. Non-trivial as a generic variational circuit, but contains no QSVT/QSP, no LCU block-encoding, no OAA, and no Trotterized Hamiltonian-simulation structure that the paper actually uses; this is by design for the ansatz_stretch proxy.

### ❌ Metadata-consistent

Cohort/task identifies this triple as label_id 'SD12', but circuit.py docstring, register(label=...), register_accounting(label=...), QuantumCircuit names, and instance.json ('instance_id': 'SD11_v3_proxy_v1', 'label': 'SD11') all use 'SD11'. paper_id, silo, and algorithm_family are otherwise consistent with the v2 extraction.

## Concerns

- Circuit and instance are labeled 'SD11' throughout while the cohort entry is SD12 (paper_id cb8d976cb90c) - label collision/mislabel risk in any join keyed on 'label'.
- Implementation is a generic RealAmplitudes ansatz; it shares no structural component with the paper's LCHS+block-encoding+QSVT+QHD pipeline, so any resource numbers from this circuit are a scale placeholder, not an algorithm-faithful estimate.
- Implemented qubit count (6) is well below the paper's reported 19; defensible under the 12-qubit proxy cap but should not be cited as a paper-scale estimate without that caveat.

## Recommendations

- Rename the SD11 identifiers in p4_experiments/experiments/silos/derivative_pricing/cb8d976cb90c/circuit.py and instance.json to SD12 (or document the SD11<->SD12 alias explicitly) before any downstream aggregation keyed on label.
- When reporting Phase-8 resource numbers for SD12, flag this entry as 'template-proxy, paper algorithm not implemented' so QHD/block-encoding cost is not inferred from a 6-qubit RealAmplitudes ansatz.
