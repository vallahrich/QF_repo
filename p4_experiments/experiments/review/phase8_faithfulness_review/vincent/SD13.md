## Verdict summary

Explicitly-declared generic RealAmplitudes template proxy at half the paper's reported qubit count for the wave-equation experiment; family bucket and scale within tolerance, but instance.json carries the wrong label tag ('SD14') and the circuit has none of the paper's QHD/LCHS/block-encoding structure.

## Checks

### ✅ Family-faithful

Cohort and instance both tag algorithm_family='other-gate-based', which matches the catch-all bucket the paper's QHD+LCHS+QSVT pipeline is registered under. The proxy is a generic ansatz, but methodology explicitly permits a template proxy within the same family bucket.

### ✅ Scale-faithful

Paper exp_2 (wave equation) reports 12 total block-encoding qubits; circuit runs at n_qubits=6, exactly the factor-of-2 boundary and well within the documented 12-qubit tractability cap.

- `v2_n_qubits`: `12`
- `circuit_n_qubits`: `6`

### ✅ Structurally non-trivial

Bare circuit is a 3-layer linear-entanglement RealAmplitudes (non-trivial parameterized ansatz); full variant adds compute-uncompute oracle proxy and measurement. Lacks paper-specific QSP/LCU/QSVT/OAA structure, but instance.json explicitly declares this as a template proxy and the project methodology permits proxies for non-variational papers.

### ❌ Metadata-consistent

instance.json sets label='SD14' and instance_id='SD14_v5_proxy_v1', and circuit.py registers label='SD14' with description/comments referencing SD14, while the cohort entry under audit is SD13. Paper_id, experiment_id, silo, and family fields are otherwise consistent. The instance notes also acknowledge that the covered v2/v3/v4 label for this paper_id corresponds to a different experiment row.

## Concerns

- Label drift: instance.json and circuit.py register this artifact as 'SD14' rather than 'SD13'; downstream registry lookups keyed on label_id may miss this experiment.
- Algorithm proxy is a plain RealAmplitudes ansatz that does not exercise any LCHS/QSVT/OAA/block-encoding primitives the paper centers on; resource estimates derived from it will systematically under-represent the paper's true gate/depth profile.
- Paper exp_2 is a wave-equation material-design problem in derivative_pricing silo; classification as 'derivative-pricing' is loose (the Black-Scholes companion experiment is the derivative-pricing one).
- n_qubits=6 omits the design-variable register entirely (paper: M*m=6 design qubits plus 6 space-plus-ancilla = 12), so the proxy collapses two distinct registers.

## Recommendations

- Rename label_id in instance.json (label, instance_id) and in circuit.py (@register label, description, function/circuit names, accounting label) from 'SD14' to 'SD13' to align with the canonical cohort identifier.
- Optionally raise n_qubits to 12 (within the tractability cap) so the proxy at least matches the paper's reported total qubit count for exp_2.
- Consider adding a comment in circuit.py noting that the paper's true algorithm is QHD+LCHS+QSVT (not a variational ansatz) so future readers do not mistake the proxy for a faithful implementation.
