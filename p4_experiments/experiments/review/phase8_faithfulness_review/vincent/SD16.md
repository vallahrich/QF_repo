## Verdict summary

Generic RealAmplitudes ansatz with compute-uncompute is a defensible hybrid/variational template proxy for the paper's PQC/CPQC+AE option-pricing pipeline at paper-consistent scale.

## Checks

### ✅ Family-faithful

Cohort family 'hybrid' matches paper (PQC/CPQC trained variationally with jaxopt, combined with AE readout). RealAmplitudes ansatz is in the variational/hybrid bucket; explicit ansatz_stretch template proxy methodology applies.

### ✅ Scale-faithful

v2 reports num_qubits NOT_STATED but discretization_qubits_range 3-15 with QFT v2 saturation at ~7 qubits. Circuit uses n=5, well within paper-explored range and tractable.

- `v2_n_qubits`: `None`
- `circuit_n_qubits`: `5`

### ✅ Structurally non-trivial

RealAmplitudes(reps=3, linear entanglement) followed by its inverse and measurement — non-trivial parameterised structure with 2-qubit gates, consistent with a variational PQC proxy. AE oracle structure not literally implemented (compute-uncompute stand-in), which is consistent with declared template-proxy methodology.

### ✅ Metadata-consistent

instance.algorithm_family='hybrid' matches cohort and v2 reading. Notes correctly disclose that paper algorithm (CPQC+AE / QFT pricing) is not implemented and that this is a template proxy.

## Concerns

- Paper's distinguishing components (CPQC controlled encodings, Amplitude Estimation oracle, or QFT-based Carr-Madan pricing) are not implemented; resource estimates from this proxy will not reflect AE/QFT structure.
- v2 num_qubits is NOT_STATED; n=5 is justified by the 3-15 discretization range but is an authoring choice, not a paper-pinned value.
- Paper is shared with SD14/SD15 (flagged redundant in v2 operator_notes); the three cohort entries may all collapse onto similar template proxies.

## Recommendations

- If Phase-8 needs AE-class resource scaling for this paper, consider promoting at least one of SD14/SD15/SD16 from ansatz_stretch to a QAE-style proxy (Grover oracle + inverse QFT) to better represent the AE readout cost.
- Document in cohort notes that SD14/SD15/SD16 share the same source dissertation to avoid triple-counting the paper in aggregate Phase-8 estimates.
