## Checks

### ❌ Family-faithful

Paper algorithm family is HHL + qPCA + swap-test (quantum linear systems / quantum kernel methods). Cohort tag is 'other-gate-based' and circuit registers as 'quantum-svm'; neither matches and the implemented logic is a generic variational ansatz, not an HHL-family circuit.

Paper does not state a concrete qubit count (NOT_STATED); circuit uses n_qubits=5 which is within the small-scale tractability budget and a defensible default.

- `v2_n_qubits`: `None`

### ❌ Structurally non-trivial

RealAmplitudes . RealAmplitudes^dagger compute-uncompute is structurally trivial relative to the paper: no QPE, no HHL inversion, no swap test, no density-matrix exponentiation, no kernel construction.

### ❌ Metadata-consistent

Cohort algorithm_family='other-gate-based' vs @register algorithm_family='quantum-svm' vs instance.algorithm_family='other-gate-based' vs paper's actual family (HHL + qPCA + swap-test) — three-way inconsistency, and the registered family ('quantum-svm') is misleading because no SVM-specific quantum structure is implemented.

## Concerns

- Circuit is an ansatz_stretch template proxy explicitly noted as not implementing the paper's algorithm.
- No HHL / QPE / swap-test / density-matrix exponentiation structure present, so resource estimates derived from this circuit will not reflect the paper's true cost.
- Three-way algorithm_family mismatch across cohort, instance.json, and circuit @register decorator.
- Operator note flags SQ10 as redundant with SQ8/SQ9/SQ11 (same Liu & Rebentrost paper) — risk of double-counting in cohort aggregates.

## Recommendations

- Reclassify cohort algorithm_family for SQ10 as 'quantum-linear-systems' or 'quantum-kernel-methods' and align the @register tag accordingly.
- If retained as a template proxy, document explicitly in PRE_REGISTRATION that SQ10 contributes only a generic-ansatz lower-bound, not an HHL-family resource estimate.
- Consider deduplicating SQ8/SQ9/SQ10/SQ11 (same paper) in any per-paper aggregation to avoid 4x weighting of Liu & Rebentrost 2017.
