## Verdict summary

Generic RealAmplitudes ansatz template proxy does not represent the paper's HHL-based quantum one-class SVM structure, and the implementation file is mislabeled as SQ10 throughout.

## Checks

### ❌ Family-faithful

Cohort tags algorithm_family='quantum-svm' matching the paper (quantum one-class SVM via HHL + density-matrix exponentiation of kernel K_ij=|<psi_i|psi_j>|^2). Circuit is a generic RealAmplitudes variational ansatz with compute-uncompute, which is a VQE/QML-ansatz family, not HHL/QPE-based SVM. Per audit guidance, HHL-type paper with generic ansatz proxy is the explicit family_drift case.

### ✅ Scale-faithful

Paper does not state qubit count (NOT_STATED in v2). Circuit uses n_qubits=5, within the tractable cap of 12 and a defensible small-scale stretch.

- `v2_n_qubits`: `None`
- `circuit_n_qubits`: `5`

### ❌ Structurally non-trivial

Circuit is RealAmplitudes(reps=3, linear) followed by its inverse and measurement. It contains none of the structural ingredients an SVM/HHL paper of this family requires: no QPE block, no controlled rotations gated on eigenvalue register, no swap test, no density-matrix-exponentiation-style controlled-SWAP cascades. Compute-uncompute of a fixed-parameter ansatz reduces to ~identity (up to ordering/seed) and is not a meaningful proxy of HHL-SVM structure.

### ❌ Metadata-consistent

File documents itself as SQ10 in docstring, @register(label='SQ10', ...), function/QuantumCircuit names ('SQ10_bare','SQ10_full'), and register_accounting(label='SQ10'). instance.json also has instance_id='SQ10_v3_proxy_v1', label='SQ10', experiment_id='exp_1', algorithm_family='other-gate-based', and algorithm_variant_paper='quantum kernel PCA ...'. None of this matches the SQ11 / exp_4 / quantum-svm cohort entry under audit.

## Concerns

- Family drift: HHL-based one-class SVM proxied by generic RealAmplitudes ansatz, no HHL/QPE/swap-test structure.
- Compute-uncompute of a fixed-parameter ansatz is ~identity and offers no structural fidelity to the paper's algorithm.
- Label mismatch: circuit.py and instance.json both identify as SQ10 (exp_1, kernel PCA, 'other-gate-based'), but cohort row is SQ11 (exp_4, quantum-svm).
- instance.algorithm_family ('other-gate-based') disagrees with cohort.algorithm_family ('quantum-svm') and with @register algorithm_family ('quantum-svm').
- Operator note in v2 flags SQ8/SQ9/SQ10/SQ11 as 4 redundant labels for the same Liu&Rebentrost theory paper; resource-cost rows from SQ11 will double-count this paper unless deduplicated.

## Recommendations

- Either (a) relabel/copy the file to SQ11 with @register(label='SQ11', experiment_id='exp_4', algorithm_family='quantum-svm') and a matching SQ11 instance.json, or (b) drop SQ11 from the cohort as a duplicate of SQ8/SQ9/SQ10 per the operator note.
- If kept, document explicitly in PRE_REGISTRATION/notes that SQ11's circuit is a generic ansatz proxy and therefore its Phase-8 resource estimates should not be interpreted as estimates of HHL-SVM cost.
- Consider replacing the compute-uncompute oracle with a minimal HHL-shaped proxy (QPE on a small Hermitian, controlled rotation, inverse QPE) so the proxy is at least structurally in the HHL family, even at toy scale.
