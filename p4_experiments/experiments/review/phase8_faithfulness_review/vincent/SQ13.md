## Verdict summary

Generic RealAmplitudes ansatz template proxy at 5 qubits for a 16-qubit IMPS state-preparation paper; family bucket matches and proxy use is the explicit methodology, but circuit-internal labels say SQ12 while the cohort entry is SQ13.

## Checks

### ✅ Family-faithful

Both paper and circuit are tagged algorithm_family='other-gate-based'. Paper is custom MPS/TTN/HTN amplitude-encoded state preparation with KAK-based 2-CNOT decomposition; circuit is a generic ansatz template proxy. Within the explicitly permitted template-proxy methodology, the family bucket is preserved.

### ❌ Scale-faithful

Paper reports up to ~16 qubits; circuit runs at n_qubits=5. Ratio is 3.2x (>factor of 2) and 5 is well below the 12-qubit tractability cap, so scale is not at the maximum allowed. Acceptable as an intentional small-scale stretch but flagged.

- `v2_n_qubits`: `16`
- `circuit_n_qubits`: `5`

### ✅ Structurally non-trivial

Bare circuit is RealAmplitudes(n=5, reps=3, entanglement='linear') with seeded parameters; full variant adds ansatz . ansatz^dagger compute-uncompute and measurement. Non-trivial as a generic state-prep proxy, though it does not contain SVD/MPS/HTN structure or the KAK 2-CNOT decomposition that defines the paper's algorithm.

### ❌ Metadata-consistent

circuit.py registers label='SQ12' (in @register, register_accounting, names 'SQ12_bare'/'SQ12_full', and module docstring), and instance.json has instance_id='SQ12_v3_proxy_v1', label='SQ12'. Cohort/task label_id is SQ13. Paper_id (59cca9744140), silo, and algorithm_family align, but the SQ12/SQ13 label mismatch is a real metadata inconsistency.

## Concerns

- circuit.py and instance.json both label this artifact 'SQ12' while the cohort task is SQ13
- circuit is a generic RealAmplitudes ansatz; it does not implement IMPS, SVD-derived 4th-order unitaries, TTN/HTN connectivity, or the KAK 2-CNOT decomposition that are the paper's core contributions
- n_qubits=5 is below the 12-qubit tractability cap and not within a factor of 2 of the paper's 16
- U_depth values from paper (1,3,5,10,11) are not reflected; ansatz_layers=3 is a generic proxy depth
- paper is a methods/state-prep paper (operator note flags 'methods-only, not finance application'), so any downstream Phase-8 cost extrapolation should treat this cohort with caution

## Recommendations

- Rename label='SQ12' -> 'SQ13' in circuit.py @register, register_accounting, circuit names, and docstring; rename instance.json label/instance_id accordingly to align with the cohort label_id
- Consider lifting n_qubits toward the 12-qubit cap to better cover the paper's 16-qubit regime, or document the 5-qubit choice as an explicit stretch in instance.json notes
- If feasible, add a minimal MPS/TTN-shaped block (e.g., staircase of 2-qubit unitaries) so the proxy at least exhibits the paper's tensor-network connectivity pattern instead of pure RealAmplitudes
- Flag this cohort as 'methods-only / not finance application' in the Phase-8 cost aggregator to avoid biasing finance-task resource estimates
