## Verdict summary

Generic RealAmplitudes ansatz_stretch proxy at paper-scale 11 qubits; family-faithful as a quantum-ml variational PQC, but omits paper-specific QBGU/QMTL share-and-specify and SWAP-test loss structure.

## Checks

### ✅ Family-faithful

Paper is a parametrised QNN/PQC with hardware-efficient layered RY/RZ/CNOT ansatz; RealAmplitudes (RY + linear CX) is a recognised hardware-efficient ansatz in the same quantum-ml family.

### ✅ Scale-faithful

Circuit n_qubits=11 matches v2 num_qubits=11 (canonical override from P3) exactly.

- `v2_n_qubits`: `11`
- `circuit_n_qubits`: `11`

### ✅ Structurally non-trivial

Bare uses ansatz_stretch (RealAmplitudes-style); full performs ansatz . ansatz^dagger compute-uncompute then computational-basis measurement. Non-trivial parametrised entangling structure with measurement.

### ❌ Metadata-consistent

instance.parameters.ansatz_layers=3 disagrees with v2 ansatz_layers=4 (paper L=4); instance.notes and circuit docstring acknowledge proxy status. Minor parameter drift, not structural.

## Concerns

- ansatz_layers=3 in instance vs L=4 reported in paper / v2 extraction.
- Proxy does not implement QBGU, share-and-specify QMTL, SWAP-test fidelity loss, or label/ancilla qubit partitioning described by the paper.
- SQ14/SQ15 flagged as redundant by operator note; both rebound as generic ansatz_stretch proxies.

## Recommendations

- Bump ansatz_layers to 4 in instance.json to match paper L=4 (cheap fix, no structural change).
- Document in notes that proxy intentionally collapses QSTL/QMTL distinction (acceptable for resource-stretch role), to make redundancy with SQ14 explicit downstream.
