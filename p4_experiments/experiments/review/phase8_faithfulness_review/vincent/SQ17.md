## Verdict summary

Amplitude-encoding paired-inverse proxy is a defensible quantum-ml family proxy for the Quantum Sparsitron/Ising paper at tractable scale, but the circuit/instance carry a stale 'SQ3' label rather than SQ17 and the proxy omits the AE/AA/QIPE oracle structure the paper actually relies on.

## Checks

### ✅ Family-faithful

Cohort algorithm_family=quantum-ml matches v2 encoding=amplitude and the paper's Quantum Sparsitron / amplitude-encoded weight states; circuit registers under quantum-ml-finance / quantum-ml.

### ✅ Scale-faithful

Paper does not state a concrete qubit count (NOT_STATED); circuit uses n_qubits=5, well within the 12-qubit tractability cap and a reasonable small-scale proxy.

- `v2_n_qubits`: `None`
- `circuit_n_qubits`: `5`

### ✅ Structurally non-trivial

Bare circuit composes a multi-layer amplitude encoder with measurements; full variant adds encoder . encoder^dagger paired-inverse for ACAE-style oracle accounting. This is non-trivial and matches the paper's amplitude-encoding primitive, though it does not implement the full AE/AA/quantum-inner-product-estimation Sparsitron pipeline (acknowledged as a template proxy).

### ❌ Metadata-consistent

Circuit @register label='SQ3', instance.json label='SQ3' and instance_id='SQ3_v3_proxy_v1', but this audit target is label_id=SQ17 (paper_id and experiment_id match). v2 operator_notes also flags 'SQ16/SQ17 redundant' (same Rebentrost paper). Stale SQ3 tag is a labelling/registry inconsistency, not a logic error.

## Concerns

- Circuit/instance carry label 'SQ3' instead of 'SQ17'; downstream registry lookups by label_id=SQ17 will not resolve to this circuit unless aliased.
- Template proxy implements only amplitude encoding + paired inverse; it does not exercise amplitude estimation, amplitude amplification, quantum minimum-finding, or quantum inner-product estimation that the paper's Quantum Sparsitron and Quantum Hedge results depend on.
- v2 extraction itself flags SQ16/SQ17 as redundant entries on the same Rebentrost paper; double-counting risk in cohort aggregates.

## Recommendations

- Re-tag circuit @register label and instance.json label/instance_id to 'SQ17' (or add an explicit alias mapping SQ17 -> SQ3 proxy) to keep registry lookups consistent.
- If higher fidelity is desired later, extend the proxy with an amplitude-estimation oracle wrapper to better mirror the paper's AE-based subroutines.
- Resolve SQ16/SQ17 redundancy at the cohort level (drop one or document the intentional duplicate) before aggregating Phase-8 resource estimates.
