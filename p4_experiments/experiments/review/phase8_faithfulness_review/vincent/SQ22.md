## Verdict summary

Generic RealAmplitudes ansatz_stretch proxy at n=5 for an SI-PQC/WDM paper whose qubit count is NOT_STATED; defensible template proxy under the declared methodology, but does not implement MEDL/WDM/QSVT/LCU structure.

## Checks

### ✅ Family-faithful

Cohort family 'other-gate-based' matches the paper's variational/hybrid SI-PQC class; a parameterized ansatz is in the same broad bucket as a parameterized SI-PQC, even though the paper's specific construction (MEDL+WDM via QSVT/LCU) is not implemented.

### ✅ Scale-faithful

Paper does not state n; v2 num_qubits=NOT_STATED. Circuit n_qubits=5 is a reasonable Tier-1 small-scale proxy and within tractability cap of 12.

- `v2_n_qubits`: `None`
- `circuit_n_qubits`: `5`

### ❌ Structurally non-trivial

Circuit is a generic RealAmplitudes(reps=3, linear) plus a compute-uncompute pair as the 'oracle'. It contains none of the paper's distinctive structures: no block-encoding of constraints fk, no LCU state-preparation-pair, no QSVT polynomial for exp(f), no digital->analog parameter conversion (controlled Ry) for WDM, no amplitude amplification. Acceptable only because the cohort methodology explicitly permits ansatz-stretch template proxies.

### ✅ Metadata-consistent

instance.json, circuit.py docstring, and register_accounting all clearly disclose this is an ansatz_stretch template proxy that does NOT implement the paper's algorithm; algorithm_variant_paper correctly identifies WDM within SI-PQC; paper_claimed.num_qubits=null matches v2 NOT_STATED.

## Concerns

- Structural fidelity is low: none of MEDL, WDM, QSVT, LCU, block-encoding, amplitude amplification, or digital->analog parameter conversion is present.
- Paper's qubit count is unspecified, so scale-faithfulness cannot be empirically anchored beyond 'small and tractable'.
- Resource estimates derived from this circuit will reflect a generic 5-qubit RealAmplitudes ansatz, not the SI-PQC depth scaling O(n M df log(1/(F eps))) the paper claims.

## Recommendations

- Keep the DRIFT_MINOR classification visible in downstream Phase-8 aggregation so SI-PQC/QSVT/LCU papers are not interpreted as faithful implementations.
- If a higher-fidelity proxy is later authored, consider an LCU-shaped template (state-preparation-pair + controlled-unitary block) plus a small QSVT-style polynomial block to better reflect MEDL structure.
- Document at the cohort level that SQ21/SQ22/SQ23 share a paper and all use ansatz_stretch proxies, to avoid double-counting structural drift.
