## Verdict summary

Generic RealAmplitudes ansatz_stretch proxy at correct paper scale (6 qubits) and within the quantum-ml family; does not implement the paper's RZ/RX/RZZ qopula gate structure or QCBM SPSA/annealing training, and instance algorithm_variant_paper labels it 'regular training' while v2 records the SPSA-annealing QCBM variant.

## Checks

### ✅ Family-faithful

Paper is a QCBM (quantum generative model, variational ansatz) in the quantum-ml family; RealAmplitudes parametrized ansatz proxy is family-consistent.

### ✅ Scale-faithful

Circuit uses 6 qubits, matching the paper's primary 6-qubit (3 qubits/variable) qopula configuration; ansatz_layers proxy = 3 vs paper's primary 8 (paper sweeps [4,8,16]) is within the template-proxy stretch budget.

- `v2_n_qubits`: `6`
- `circuit_n_qubits`: `6`

### ✅ Structurally non-trivial

Bare circuit is a parametrised RealAmplitudes ansatz with linear entanglement; full variant adds an ansatz . ansatz^dagger compute-uncompute pair plus measurement. Non-trivial parametrised structure suitable as a QCBM-class ansatz proxy, though it does not reproduce the paper's qopula RZ/RX/RZZ block.

### ❌ Metadata-consistent

instance.json algorithm_variant_paper says 'QCBM with regular training' but the v2 extraction (operator_notes / cost_function / annealing fields) characterises the experiment as the SPSA QCBM-Annealing variant; cohort algorithm_family 'quantum-ml' matches the paper. Variant label is the only metadata mismatch.

## Concerns

- Implemented circuit is a generic RealAmplitudes proxy and does not realise the paper's qopula RZ/RX/RZZ per-variable block structure.
- Instance algorithm_variant_paper ('QCBM with regular training') is inconsistent with the v2 extraction's QCBM-Annealing characterisation of exp_2.
- ansatz_layers in the instance (3) is below the paper's primary depth of 8 layers; acceptable under template-proxy methodology but worth flagging.

## Recommendations

- Update instance.json algorithm_variant_paper to 'QCBM with SPSA annealing-style training (qopula ansatz)' to align with the v2 extraction.
- Optionally raise ansatz_layers to 8 to better mirror the paper's primary depth while remaining tractable at 6 qubits.
- Document in notes that the proxy substitutes RealAmplitudes for the paper's qopula RZ/RX/RZZ block.
