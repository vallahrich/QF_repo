## Verdict summary

Generic RealAmplitudes ansatz_stretch proxy at the paper-faithful 9-qubit scale is a defensible template proxy for the QCBM/qopula variational generative model in this experiment.

## Checks

### ✅ Family-faithful

Paper algorithm is QCBM (quantum-ml, variational-NISQ); circuit is a parametrised RealAmplitudes ansatz registered as algorithm_family='quantum-ml'. Same family bucket.

### ✅ Scale-faithful

v2 num_qubits=9 (3 variables x 3 qubits-per-variable, GHZ-vs-W three-variable comparison in Fig. 3b); circuit n_qubits=9. Exact match.

- `v2_n_qubits`: `9`
- `circuit_n_qubits`: `9`

### ✅ Structurally non-trivial

RealAmplitudes(reps=3, entanglement='linear') provides parametrised single-qubit rotations plus CNOT entanglers; full variant adds compute-uncompute (a . a^dagger) and computational-basis measurement, matching the variational-ansatz + measurement structure expected for a QCBM-class circuit. Does not reproduce qopula RZ/RX/RZZ per-variable block (template-proxy methodology, expected).

### ✅ Metadata-consistent

instance.json (n_qubits=9, ansatz_layers=3, paper_claimed.num_qubits=9, algorithm_family='quantum-ml') consistent with circuit.py register() tags and v2 extraction's revised num_qubits=9.

## Concerns

- Template proxy does not implement the paper's qopula-specific gate structure (RZ/RX/RZZ per-variable blocks); explicitly disclosed in instance.notes and circuit docstring.
- instance.parameters.ansatz_layers=3 differs from paper's 8 layers reported for many experiments; acceptable under ansatz_stretch template-proxy methodology but worth noting for resource-cost projections.
- v2 extraction itself flags experiment-mapping uncertainty (SQ26-SQ31 redundant); 9-qubit choice is the best defensible interpretation but not unique.

## Recommendations

- Optional: bump ansatz_layers from 3 to 8 to better match the paper's depth choice if Phase-8 cost projection is sensitive to depth.
- Optional: leave a note in cohort manifest that qopula-specific RZZ structure is intentionally not modeled.
